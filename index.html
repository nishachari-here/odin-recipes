Homemade Recipes

  • Alfredo

    Alfredo is a creamy pasta sauce made with butter, cream, and Parmesan cheese. It's rich and indulgent, perfect for a comforting meal.

  • Lemonade

    Lemonade is a refreshing drink made with lemon juice, sugar, and water. It's perfect for hot summer days.

  • French Fries

    French fries are deep-fried potatoes, crispy on the outside and soft on the inside. They're a popular side dish or snack.
